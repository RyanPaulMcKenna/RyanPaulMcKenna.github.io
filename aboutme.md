I am an Embedded Systems Engineer and PhD researcher with a unique blend of deep theoretical expertise and
 practical, hands-on engineering experience. My professional focus lies at the intersection of Embedded Linux, kernel
 driver development, and RTOS-based systems, with a strong foundation in physics, robotics, and applied machine
 learning.
 I thrive in multidisciplinary environments where software and hardware meet. Whether it’s implementing Linux device
 drivers, soldering prototypes, machining parts, or debugging with an oscilloscope, I enjoy working across the stack to
 deliver robust, integrated systems.
 Currently completing a PhD in Computer Science at the University of York—with a research focus in robotics,
 reinforcement learning, and deformable object manipulation—I bring a high level of mathematical and analytical rigor
 to engineering challenges. My passion lies in building real-world systems: from Yocto builds, device trees, and low
level firmware to schematics, PCB design, and physical prototyping
